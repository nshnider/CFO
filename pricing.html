Frequently Asked Questions
What's the difference between Starter and Pro?
Starter gives you full access to the current version of Financial Clarity at the time of purchase — forever, with no recurring fees. Pro gives you everything in Starter plus all future tools, new industry dashboards, and member blog content as we add them.
Can I try it before I buy?
Yes — try the free demo at no cost, no credit card required. The free demo includes 3 industry KPI dashboards so you can experience the tool before purchasing. Pro plans also include a 14-day free trial.
What happens when new tools are added?
Pro subscribers automatically get access to every new tool and industry dashboard as it's released — no additional cost. Starter subscribers keep the version they purchased and can upgrade to Pro at any time.
Can I cancel my Pro subscription?
Yes — cancel anytime with no penalty. Your access continues through the end of your current billing period.
Is this a replacement for a CFO or accountant?
Financial Clarity is an analytical tool, not a substitute for professional advice. For hands-on fractional CFO and advisory services, contact The Shnider Group LLC directly at nshnider@theshnidergroup.com or (614) 582-0108.
