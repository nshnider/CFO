Service firms sell time and expertise. Utilization, gross margin, and client retention are the core levers. DSO directly determines how much cash is floating in unpaid invoices.

Key relationships
Effective rate = Revenue ÷ Billable hrs
A/R tied up = Revenue × (DSO ÷ 365)
Client LTV = Avg revenue ÷ (1 − Retention)
